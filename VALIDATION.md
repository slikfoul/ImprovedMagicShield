# Improved Magic Shield 1.2.2 verification

## Automated coverage

The Release build has no compiler warnings or errors. Version 1.2.2 passed 2300 managed/installed-IL checks and 228 Unity Editor component checks. Run `./Test.ps1` for production helper and UI lifecycle checks using managed game/Unity test doubles, plus installed-game IL checks. Run `./RunVisualChecks.ps1` with Unity 6000.0.75f1 for visual controls against the production DLL and actual Unity components. The exact game assembly and compiled DLL SHA-256 are recorded in the versioned artifacts/checks report. Neither suite is a Valheim gameplay or multiplayer test.

Covered behavior includes:

- The independent health outline is off by default and runs with overhead numbers disabled. Its width uses remaining/full shield protection for players and summons, including weak full shields, fractional shields and incoming replacement capacities. Its antialiased nine-slice shell has a semicircular outer edge, a transparent square inner opening and a visible purple band. Per-height textures are shared and scale to preserve the capsule shape. A RectMask2D reveals the remaining fraction of a fixed full-width frame without placing a vertical end cap through health. Pixel checks in a real Unity camera render confirm an untouched green health center, preserved square health corners at full and partial protection, and clipping beyond the remaining fraction. Shared sprites are reused and released even when native headers are destroyed before display cleanup. It does not change native health geometry, color, fill or input. Native parent movement, bar resizing, UI reuse, hidden health/header/HUD, missing shields and cleanup are checked with real Unity components and managed doubles. Remote outlines use owner-published capacity and require its timestamp to match the protection snapshot; missing capacity, invalid values, stale samples, previous publishers and old-peer refreshes hide the outline. An actual multiplayer session and appearance alongside HUD mods remain untested.
- All requested shield replacement examples, equality, full reset of absorbed damage and time, and rejection without changing capacity, damage, time, duration, source or variant.
- Incoming skill/item-level parameters and the positive-world-level bonus. A small interpreter executes the installed game's numeric `SE_Shield.SetLevel` IL and compares the result with the mod's capacity calculation.
- Existing shield branches in both SEMan overloads; new/non-shield effects and `resetTime=false` follow vanilla.
- The installed Character refresh block is matched and transformed; its entry branch label is preserved, its source update is kept for accepted casts, and an emitted managed version executes rejection without calling SetAttacker. Changed or ambiguous hook layouts fail closed.
- The installed Character and SEMan hash/RPC paths retain vanilla ownership checks. No target-filtering, casting or ownership methods are patched.
- Disabled mechanics follow vanilla for subsequent operations without removing the active effect.
- Overhead labels always exclude Player.m_localPlayer with the lower HUD both enabled and disabled, while publishing the local shield for other clients. Tests move the rendered anchor, physical center and camera independently between 10 Hz ticks; only the rendered anchor and current camera affect positions. Native TMP captions share the character header canvas, hierarchy, font and local geometry; repeated discovery and render frames reuse them. Hidden native headers hide their captions instead of switching anchors. The world-anchor fallback is painted only from the main-camera current-frame snapshot during GUI Repaint. Secondary cameras, old frames, scaled camera pixel dimensions, distance, behind-camera positions, death, teleport, hidden HUD, cutscene, invalid network views, removed effects and cleanup are covered. Owner identity and snapshot freshness are rechecked before projection and painting, including changes between ticks and after projection. Installed-game IL confirms GetTopPoint uses TransformPoint rather than collider bounds, GameCamera updates in LateUpdate, and the compiled plugin subscribes/unsubscribes the camera callback.
- The actual local HUD implementation is exercised with UI test doubles: native canvas parent, a fixed bottom-center row independent of stamina/Eitr/adrenaline bounds, proportional fill, current capacity after recast, independent toggle, death, teleport, hidden HUD, no shield, no local player, HUD recreation and cleanup.

## Remaining live checks

1. With the mod enabled, cast on yourself and verify the damage/recast examples in README, including equality and a weaker refused cast near expiry. Confirm the refused cast does not extend duration.
2. Toggle Enabled off with a shield active. Confirm the effect remains, subsequent casts follow vanilla, and both mod displays disappear. Re-enable without resetting saved display settings.
3. Leave overhead labels off and enable only the local bar. Verify current remaining/full values and fill after damage, accepted replacement, rejected replacement, expiry and break. Test death/respawn, teleport, main menu, hidden HUD and HUD recreation.
4. Check native HUD layout with stamina, Eitr and adrenaline visible together, at different resolutions, HUD scales and controller layouts. The shield uses its own fixed native-canvas row and does not follow resource visibility, animation or layout changes. Other HUD mods can occupy that space; live compatibility is unverified.
5. Enable overhead labels separately and change their scale. Verify that no overhead label appears on your own character with the local bar either on or off. Walk, run, turn the camera and move together with another shielded character; compare label motion with the visible character and bubble. Other-character owner-side values should update after damage, and remote values should follow actual ZDO replication. The owner publishes changed values at most ten times per second and a heartbeat twice per second while shielded. Expire stale data after two seconds and invalidate data from a previous owner.
6. Use two modded clients with different skill/item levels. Cast on each other in both orders and verify the recipient owner's outcome. Test eligible creatures with server ownership and peer ownership changes. An unmodified recipient remains vanilla; a server-only installation cannot change that player's shield processing.
7. With Magic Shield Effect off, confirm no appearance change. Turn off EffectsControl's Magic shield section, enable this mod's visual block, and adjust all five controls on existing and new shields. Test toggling both mods' controls, animated light fading, remote shields, shield hit/break effects and disabling/unloading without a recast. Confirm damage, duration and colliders are unaffected in gameplay.

## Compatibility boundary

The strength calculation matches the inspected installed vanilla SE_Shield.SetLevel. Another mod that changes shield calculation or the same refresh blocks needs compatibility review. The mod adds no custom RPC or version enforcement and does not synchronize the status effect itself. Overhead numbers are display-only owner-published data.

## Source and release status

This repository contains source, tests, build/package/install tools and documentation. Tagged source and binary packages are distributed through GitHub Releases. The archive manifest links to the actual GitHub repository. Building or registering a local package does not publish it; Thunderstore publication requires a separate explicit command.

Native label placement is also exercised against real Unity RectTransform corners and installed EnemyHud private fields, including hidden headers and canvas scaling. The installed ConfigurationManager reads the master switch priority above all five sliders. Managed checks cover enlarged labels, moving/recreated headers, independently hidden names/health and the world-anchor fallback.

Capacity-based HUD width is checked for 200, 365 and 700 protection, full fill at every maximum, fractional segment widths, live segment-scale changes, damage without frame resizing and independence from other resource widths. Label checks include player and summoned-skeleton placement above native names, larger fonts growing upward, and positive/negative offsets with canvas scaling.

The segment regression covers every integer setting from 10 through 500 for 100, 200, 365 and 700 protection. The frame width is independent of the caption's preferred width; short frames retain a centered, unwrapped caption at its native font size. Real Unity component checks cover native TMP caption reuse, hierarchy-following motion without reprojection, name/health separation, enlarged fonts, manual offset and hiding. The user confirmed that the preceding native-header build no longer jittered in the running game. Automated checks confirm numeric-only local, native-header and fallback captions. This build adds a fixed resource row and activity-based fading; its final appearance and activity detection still need a live check.

HUD activity checks cover five seconds of display followed by a half-second fade, full and damaged shields, shield damage revealing a hidden bar, alerted-enemy target reports maintaining visibility, no reveal from attack/block/bow inputs or generic/non-alerted targeting, hiding after combat, accepted same-instance full-to-full recasts, rejection without timer reset and new shield instances. Real Unity checks create the production native HUD clone with an animated source pose, verify normalization and a fixed bottom-center anchor, and exercise its CanvasGroup fade without input interception. The observer binds to the installed Player.RPC_OnTargeted method without changing its behavior. The inspected MonsterAI reports its current player target and IsAlerted state through this path; the vanilla player handler also uses the alerted flag to reset combat music.
